Rubric. Score each reply on all four: Accurate (addresses the real issues, no wrong facts) · Tone (fits the customer's mood and the urgency) · Brief (short, no filler) · No promises (no invented refunds, timelines or fixes).
3 = good on all four, ready to send after a quick check · 2 = minor issues, small edits needed · 1 = a real problem to fix before sending.
